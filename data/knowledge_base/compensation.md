# Compensation Structure

## Salary Bands by Role

### Engineering
- **Junior Software Engineer**: $90,000 - $110,000
- **Senior Software Engineer**: $130,000 - $160,000
- **ML Engineer**: $140,000 - $170,000
- **DevOps Engineer**: $120,000 - $150,000

### Sales
- **Account Executive**: $100,000 - $150,000 (includes bonus structure)
- **Sales Manager**: $120,000 - $180,000

### HR
- **HR Manager**: $85,000 - $105,000
- **HR Specialist**: $55,000 - $70,000

### Finance
- **Financial Analyst**: $95,000 - $115,000
- **Finance Manager**: $110,000 - $140,000

### Product
- **Product Manager**: $120,000 - $160,000
- **Senior Product Manager**: $150,000 - $190,000

## Benefits Package
- Health Insurance: Company covers 85% of premiums
- Dental & Vision: Company covers 100%
- 401(k): Company matches up to 4%
- Stock Options: Eligible employees receive vesting schedules
- Wellness Program: Gym membership reimbursement up to $100/month

## Performance Bonus
- Eligible employees receive annual performance bonuses
- Bonus range: 10-20% of base salary depending on performance tier
- Paid out in Q1 following the performance year

## Equity
Employees at manager level and above are eligible for stock options with standard 4-year vesting and 1-year cliff.
