# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Employment

### Q: How do I request time off?
A: You can request PTO through the HR portal. Submit requests at least 2 weeks in advance for better approval chances. Your manager will review and approve based on team availability.

### Q: What is the company's attendance policy?
A: We have a flexible attendance policy. Employees are expected to be present during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM) and maintain communication with their teams.

### Q: How do I report a workplace issue?
A: You can report issues to your manager, HR department, or through our anonymous hotline. All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially.

## Benefits & Compensation

### Q: When am I eligible for benefits?
A: Full-time employees are eligible for benefits immediately. Part-time employees after 6 months of employment.

### Q: How much does the company contribute to my 401(k)?
A: The company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of your salary. There is no vesting period for employer contributions.

### Q: Can I change my health insurance plan?
A: Yes, during open enrollment periods (typically November). You can also change plans if you experience a qualifying life event.

### Q: What parental leave do I get?
A: Primary parents get 12 weeks paid leave. Secondary parents get 6 weeks. Adoption assistance provides up to $10,000.

## Professional Development

### Q: Is tuition reimbursement available?
A: Yes, up to $5,000 per year for work-related education. You must complete the program and maintain employment for 1 year after completion.

### Q: Can I attend conferences?
A: Conference attendance is reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Contact your manager and HR for approval and budget allocation.

### Q: Does the company offer professional certifications?
A: Yes, we support industry-relevant certifications. Work with your manager to identify relevant certifications and budget allocation.

## Workplace Policies

### Q: What is the remote work policy?
A: We support flexible remote work arrangements. Most teams work 2-3 days in office, 2-3 days remote. Specific arrangements vary by team and role.

### Q: What is the dress code?
A: Business casual for office days. Casual dress is fine for remote work.

### Q: How are performance reviews conducted?
A: Annual performance reviews are conducted in Q1. They include self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback.

## IT & Security

### Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the company SSO portal or contact IT support.

### Q: What should I do if I suspect a security breach?
A: Report immediately to the IT security team or call the security hotline.

### Q: Can I install software on my company laptop?
A: No. All software must be approved by IT and installed through the official management system.
