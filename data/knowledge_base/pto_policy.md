# PTO Policy

## Overview
All full-time employees are entitled to Paid Time Off (PTO) to promote work-life balance and employee wellness.

## Annual Entitlement
- **New Employees (0-2 years)**: 15 days per year
- **Established Employees (2-5 years)**: 18 days per year
- **Senior Employees (5+ years)**: 20 days per year

## Accrual
PTO accrues on a monthly basis. Employees accrue 1.25 days per month for new hires, 1.5 days for established, and 1.67 days for senior employees.

## Carryover
- Employees can carry over up to 5 days to the next calendar year
- Carryover days must be used within the first quarter of the following year
- Days beyond the 5-day limit are forfeited

## Request Process
1. Submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal
2. Manager approval required
3. Requests are approved on a first-come, first-served basis
4. Team coverage must be maintained

## Emergency Leave
In case of emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible. Documentation may be required for extended absences.

## Payout
Upon termination, unused PTO is paid out at the employee's current salary rate.
