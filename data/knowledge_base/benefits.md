# Employee Benefits Guide

## Health & Wellness

### Medical Insurance
- PPO Plan: Employee pays 15% of premium
- HMO Plan: Employee pays 10% of premium
- Company covers 85% of premium costs

### Dental Coverage
- Basic: Cleaning, exams (100% covered)
- Major: Fillings, crowns (80% covered)
- Orthodontics: 50% covered, max $2000/year

### Vision Insurance
- Eye exams: 100% covered, once per year
- Frames/Contacts: 50% covered, up to $200/year
- Prescription lenses: 100% covered

### Mental Health
- EAP (Employee Assistance Program): 6 free counseling sessions/year
- Mental health therapy: Covered under medical plan

## Retirement

### 401(k) Plan
- Immediate eligibility
- Company match: 100% up to 4% of salary
- Catch-up contributions available for age 50+

### Pension
- Defined benefit plan for eligible employees
- Vests over 5 years

## Work-Life Balance

### Flexible Work
- Remote work options (flexible arrangement)
- Flexible hours with manager approval
- Sabbatical program after 5 years service

### Family Benefits
- Parental leave: 12 weeks paid (primary parent), 6 weeks (secondary parent)
- Adoption assistance: Up to $10,000
- Childcare subsidy: Up to $250/month

## Professional Development

### Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to $5,000 per year for approved programs
- Must be work-related
- Requires minimum 1-year service after completion

### Training & Development
- Annual training budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance support: Case-by-case
- Internal training programs: Free access

## Other Benefits

### Insurance
- Life Insurance: 2x salary (company paid)
- Disability Insurance: 60% salary replacement
- Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax commuter programs

### Wellness Programs
- Gym membership: Reimbursement up to $100/month
- Wellness coaching: Free annual health screening
- On-site fitness classes: Free access

### Time Off
- Holidays: 10 federal holidays
- Sick leave: 10 days per year
- Volunteer time: 5 days per year
